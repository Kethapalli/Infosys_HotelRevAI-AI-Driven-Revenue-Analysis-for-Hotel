# 🏨 Hotel Revenue Strategy & Pricing Optimization Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes historical revenue, occupancy trends, pricing effectiveness, and upsell opportunities to build a data-driven hotel revenue strategy dashboard.
The solution identifies:

1.High-performing seasons and demand peaks

2.Room category performance

3.Pricing efficiency across seasons

4.Upsell potential (Spa, Dining, Premium Services)

5.High ADR and high RevPAR segments

6.Revenue contribution by booking source and branch

## 📊 Key Insights
## 1️⃣ Seasonal Demand & Revenue Trends

Identified peak seasons with highest occupancy and revenue growth.

Detected low-demand months requiring promotional pricing strategies.

Clear correlation observed between occupancy spikes and seasonal travel patterns.

## 2️⃣ Room Category Performance

Premium room types (Deluxe/Suite) generated higher ADR and RevPAR.

Standard rooms showed higher occupancy but lower revenue per available room.

Opportunity identified for strategic room upgrade promotions.

## 3️⃣ Pricing Effectiveness

High ADR during peak seasons maintained strong occupancy levels.

In low seasons, price sensitivity increased — discount strategies improved occupancy.

Optimal pricing tiers suggested based on seasonal elasticity.
